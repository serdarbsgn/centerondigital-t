External Attack Surface Analysis and Cyber Threat Intelligence Platform

SURFACEMON is the external attack surface management and cyber threat intelligence platform we built ourselves. Starting from your domain, it finds your internet-facing assets, monitors them with regular scans and brings vulnerabilities, leaked passwords and look-alike domains together on one dashboard.

  • Starts from your domain alone, nothing to install
  • Alert rules for expiring certificates and newly leaked passwords
