About RevBits

RevBits is a US-based cybersecurity company that offers innovative security solutions to give organizations the highest level of protection against cyber threats.

With advanced technologies in email security, endpoint protection, access management, network visibility and threat intelligence, RevBits enables organizations to build an integrated security architecture.

In particular, the RevBits Cyber Intelligence Platform (CIP) brings different security layers together under one roof, giving security operations central control, deep visibility and speed.

At Center On Digital, we are proud to be the authorized RevBits partner in Türkiye. We offer organizations RevBits’ award-winning security solutions together with local support and expert consulting.

Our goal is to make our customers more resilient against cyber threats and to help their digital security investments deliver the greatest possible value.

RevBits Cyber Intelligence Platform (CIP)

Managing multiple security tools is time-consuming. When security solutions don’t share information, the chance to defend proactively against different threats is lost. Built on our superior, patented technology, the RevBits Cyber Intelligence Platform combines the power of multiple security solutions and takes XDR to full-speed security. By sharing threat intelligence from several security modules and presenting it in one integrated view, the platform delivers superior customer protection.

XDR at Full Speed

Managing several security solutions takes time, and a lack of coordinated threat intelligence can be devastating for an environment. Where five separate native solutions once left gaps in coordination, the RevBits Cyber Intelligence Platform now provides integrated cybersecurity across the threat landscape, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

Cyber Intelligence with RevBits

All RevBits security modules are integrated into a single Cyber Intelligence Platform to make information sharing between modules easier, improve detection and shorten response times. This is achieved with standardized logging techniques across every one of our security modules.

XDR Powered by Multiple Modules, Managed from a Single Dashboard

The RevBits Cyber Intelligence Platform draws on the power of the advanced security modules in its unique security suite and brings them together in a single user dashboard. With single sign-on, system administrators can monitor, interact with and control security solutions such as Endpoint Security, Email Security, Privileged Access Management, Deception Technology and Zero Trust Network.

Deception Technology

Defend Your Network with Deception Technology

Threat actors have become extremely sophisticated and use many tactics to evade defensive software. Corporate networks are larger and more complex than ever. RevBits Deception Technology protects against security breaches by deceiving even the most skilled attackers.

Once attackers enter a corporate network, they don’t realize that the real IT and OT assets, data and infrastructure are being protected by steering them toward honeypots that look legitimate.

RevBits: A World Leader in Cyber Deception Technology

RevBits Deception Technology brings an advanced architecture to the deception/honeypot market and increases security administrators’ threat-hunting capacity. When real server-based honeypots are deployed in a resource-rich environment, telling real servers from decoys becomes nearly impossible. Add the ability to plant fake honeypot credentials across the network, and points of breach are exposed and isolated.

Deception Technology with RevBits

RevBits Deception Technology is designed to attract, trap and hold malicious actions or malware that enter the network looking for valuable assets. Because RevBits deploys real server-based traps, it is easier to tell genuine activity from malicious software. Using RevBits’ integrated solutions enables intelligence exchange between modules based on standardized logging, which improves detection, response time and protection for network assets, honeypots included. Double virtualization strengthens security and makes the honeypots inescapable once they are accessed.

Email Security

Advanced Email Security for Persistent Threats

True cybersecurity requires closing the gaps in email security. RevBits Email Security closes those gaps with its unique design: because it combines a strong Secure Email Gateway with a unique client-based inbox analysis agent, malicious emails cannot escape detection.

Go Beyond the Inbox with a Secure Email Gateway

Stop email security threats before they reach the endpoint with the RevBits Secure Email Gateway.

More than 90% of cyber attacks start with an email, and phishing keeps rising every year. Businesses need end-to-end protection that keeps sophisticated malicious emails out of end users’ inboxes.

A cloud-based solution, the RevBits Secure Email Gateway secures inbound, outbound and internal email for businesses with a mail transfer agent (MTA) configuration.

RevBits Email Security Strengthens Your Email Security Architecture

RevBits Email Security enhances standard email security by detecting and blocking sophisticated malicious emails that get past the outer layers of an organization’s email security stack. With RevBits Email Security, organizations can protect the employee endpoint, the final point of email interaction.

Privileged Access Management (PAM)

Manage Privileged Access

Privileged Access Management is a critical cybersecurity strategy that manages and secures elevated (privileged) accounts, processes and access to high-value IT systems. It is essential for reducing security risk while improving operational efficiency.

Why Does It Matter?

Unmanaged privileged accounts can act as a gateway to major breaches. PAM solutions control, monitor and audit every privileged interaction across servers, databases, cloud platforms and air-gapped environments to ensure accountability and protect sensitive assets.

Core Capabilities of a Modern PAM Platform

  • Unified Modules: Combines privileged access, session, password, service account, certificate, key and CI/CD secrets management in one integrated solution.
  • Jump Server Architecture: Isolates user sessions through intermediate jump servers and adds keystroke and video recording to eliminate backdoor risks.
  • Native Client Support: Provides seamless one-click access to servers and databases (RDP, SSH, Oracle, MySQL and more) without external exposure.
  • Air-Gapped Environment Support: Works flawlessly even on isolated, offline networks and strengthens the security of sensitive infrastructure.
  • Zero-Knowledge Encryption and Analytics: Protects sensitive credentials with browser-based encryption and monitors privileged behavior to detect anomalies.
  • Fast, Scalable Deployment: Built on a modern, scalable architecture that uses SSO (Active Directory, LDAP, Kerberos) and can be ready within days, with no endpoint agents.

Zero Trust Network (ZTN)

Secure Network Access Beyond VPN

Extend network security to the endpoints with confidence using RevBits Zero Trust Network (ZTN). Access by remote employees and third-party contractors to the resources and applications they need on the network is properly controlled through direct user authentication.

It’s time to take remote access security beyond VPN encryption and stop open network access.

Zero Trust Network with RevBits

The remote workforce, BYOD assets and third-party access have created a new security dilemma for organizations. Relying on a VPN alone to secure remote employees and third-party access is not enough to protect the network. VPNs offer some protection by encrypting incoming traffic, but the weakness is that they grant users full network access without limiting privileged access.

RevBits ZTN encrypts external users over SSL/TLS, authenticates them, and connects them securely only to the internal network assets and applications they are authorized to reach, without granting full network access.
